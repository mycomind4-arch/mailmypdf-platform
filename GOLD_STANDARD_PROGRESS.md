## Current hard gates

- Missing or unverified evidence stays explicit and blocks consequential execution.
- A workflow cannot claim capability merely because it appears in a catalog or manifest.
- Validation must pass before review, approval, mailing, tracking, and proof certification.
- Approval and mailing remain explicit runtime stages; they cannot be inferred from a draft or schedule.
- Tests must exercise representative fixtures and regression cases before a workflow can be certified.

## Next execution order

1. Bring Small Business workflows through the same strict lifecycle contract.
2. Bring government/administrative workflows through the same contract and registry diagnostics.
3. Add representative fixtures and regression gates for each workflow family.
4. Close real persistence, fulfillment, tracking, proof, authorization, and deployment gaps rather than marking them complete by documentation alone.
5. Finish remaining verticals and then run ecosystem-wide deployed smoke certification.
